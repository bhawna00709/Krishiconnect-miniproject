KrishiConnect Project Documentation & Framework

1. Project Introduction

Agriculture is an important source of livelihood in rural areas and plays a major role in the local economy. However, many agricultural lands remain unused when landowners move to cities for education, employment or other reasons, or when they are unable to cultivate their land themselves. At the same time, many people are interested in farming but do not have direct access to suitable agricultural land.

KrishiConnect is a web-based platform designed to connect such landowners with farmers or cultivators and help make unused agricultural land productive. The platform focuses on two main pillars:

  • Connecting landowners with farmers for the productive use of fallow or unused agricultural land.
  • Providing farmers with real-time market-price information and a direct digital marketplace for fruits, vegetables, flowers, grains, and pure dairy.

2. Problem Statement & Objectives

Problem Statement: Agricultural land can remain underutilized when owners migrate away from rural areas. In parallel, individuals interested in farming face difficulty finding suitable land. Informal arrangements lack clearly recorded terms relating to duration, responsibilities, payment, and produce ownership. Furthermore, small producers struggle to reach consumers directly.
Key Project Objectives Implemented in this Website:
Separate login/registration for 4 roles
Agricultural land listing & management
Cultivator search & filter by soil/irrigation
Proposal workflow between parties
Structured land-use agreement module
Produce marketplace with shopping cart
Mandi price discovery based on e-NAM
Admin verification and moderation desk
